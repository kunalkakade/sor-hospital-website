निदानापासून शस्त्रक्रिया, किमोथेरपी व पॅलिएटिव्ह केअरपर्यंत — सर्व सेवा निरामय हॉस्पिटल, अकोला येथे.From diagnosis to surgery, chemotherapy and palliative care — every service in one place at Niramay Hospital, Akola.

Second opinion partnership

Second opinion from Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai

Niramay Hospital, Akola has a tie-up with Tata Memorial Hospital through Navya, so you can get an online second opinion directly from Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai — without travelling.

  • Comprehensive cancer diagnosis

    Coordinated diagnostics — specialist OPD consultation, imaging, biopsy, pathology, CT scan, digital X-ray, laboratory services, 2D echocardiography and cancer staging. Niramay has its own CT scan centre, with a PET-CT tie-up for accurate staging.

  • Advanced cancer surgery

    A wide range of cancer surgeries using evidence-based techniques for complete tumour removal while preserving function — breast, oral, head & neck, thyroid, gastrointestinal, colorectal, liver, pancreatic, soft-tissue sarcoma, skin and gynaecological cancers. Equipped with a laparoscopy unit for minimally invasive surgery, with special expertise in head & neck free-flap reconstruction — 500+ free-flap surgeries and many HIPEC procedures for ovarian cancer.

  • Chemotherapy

    A dedicated day-care and inpatient chemotherapy unit following internationally accepted protocols. Each plan is individualised to the cancer type, stage and the patient's health, with careful monitoring to maximise benefit and minimise side effects.

  • Immunotherapy & targeted therapy

    Advanced treatments for selected cancers that help the immune system fight cancer or target cancer cells specifically, while sparing healthy tissue.

  • Multidisciplinary cancer care

    Every case is reviewed by a team across surgical oncology, medical oncology, pathology, radiology, anaesthesia, critical care, nutrition, physiotherapy and palliative care — for the most appropriate treatment.

  • Palliative & supportive care

    Improving quality of life by managing pain, symptoms, nutrition and emotional well-being for patients with advanced cancer, while supporting their families. We believe in adding life to days, not just days to life.

  • Cancer screening & early detection

    Screening for breast, oral, cervical, colorectal and prostate cancers — early diagnosis improves outcomes and survival. We use an iBreast scanner for early breast cancer detection and an EVA scanner for on-site cervical cancer screening.

  • Second opinion & treatment planning

    A comprehensive review of diagnosis, pathology, imaging and previous records, with evidence-based recommendations to help you make informed decisions.

  • Diagnostic services

    Comprehensive diagnostics under one roof — CT scan, ultrasound, digital X-ray, pathology lab, ECG and 2D echocardiography.

  • Intensive care & inpatient services

    Modern operation theatres, ICU and specialised inpatient services for safe perioperative care during complex cancer treatment.

  • Cancer awareness & prevention

    Tobacco-cessation counselling, lifestyle guidance, awareness programmes and education on early warning signs to encourage timely diagnosis.
